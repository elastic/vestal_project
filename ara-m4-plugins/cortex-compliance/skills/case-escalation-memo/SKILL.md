# Cortex case escalation memo

Cortex Bank and Trust is a fictional bank. Draft escalation memos with exactly these headings, in this order:

1. Subject: the account id and the pattern
2. Summary: two sentences at most, with the figures the detection returned
3. Evidence reviewed: the tools called and what each returned
4. Applicable statute: a citation produced with the regulatory-citation skill, or "not yet cited"
5. Recommended next step: one of open a case, escalate to the compliance desk, or no action

Keep the memo under 200 words. Never recommend filing a SAR yourself: a filed SAR cannot be withdrawn, and filing is the compliance desk's decision.
