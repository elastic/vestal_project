The Know Your Customer Due Diligence policy of Cortex Bank and Trust establishes the foundational requirements for identifying, verifying, and understanding the financial activities of our customers. This section serves as the critical first line of defense against money laundering, terrorist financing, fraud, and other illicit financial activities. All employees of Cortex Bank and Trust must adhere strictly to these procedures to ensure institutional compliance with statutory and regulatory obligations.

Prior to the establishment of any new business relationship, Cortex Bank and Trust requires the completion of the Customer Identification Program. For individual clients, personnel must collect, at a minimum, the full legal name, date of birth, primary residential address, and a government-issued identification number. For corporate entities, required information includes the legal entity name, primary place of business, jurisdiction of incorporation, and the identification of all ultimate beneficial owners holding an equity interest of twelve point five percent or greater. This specific twelve point five percent threshold deliberately exceeds standard baseline regulations to reflect the conservative risk appetite of Cortex Bank and Trust. Verification of these identity elements must be completed within three business days of account opening using non-documentary methods, documentary methods, or a combination of both. Failure to verify the identity of the customer within this stipulated timeframe will result in immediate account restriction followed by mandatory closure.

Upon successful identification, Cortex Bank and Trust applies a comprehensive risk-based approach to determine the appropriate level of ongoing due diligence. Every customer is assigned a dynamic risk rating of Low, Standard, or High. This rating is calculated using a proprietary algorithmic matrix evaluating the customer jurisdiction, occupation, expected volume of transactions, and the specific types of banking products utilized. For example, a retail customer employed locally with expected monthly aggregate deposits not exceeding four thousand five hundred dollars would typically receive a Low risk rating. Conversely, a corporate entity engaged in cross-border trade, particularly involving high-risk geographic corridors or handling monthly international wire transfers exceeding seventy-five thousand dollars, will automatically be escalated to a High risk rating.

Customers assigned a High risk rating are subject strictly to Enhanced Due Diligence procedures. Cortex Bank and Trust mandates that Enhanced Due Diligence files must include corroborated, independent evidence of both the overarching source of wealth and the specific source of funds. Furthermore, senior management approval, specifically from a compliance officer at the Vice President level or above, is mandatory before establishing a relationship with any High risk customer. Any politically exposed person, whether domestic or foreign, along with their immediate family members and close associates, triggers an immediate classification as High risk and requires the direct authorization of the Chief Compliance Officer. In addition, Cortex Bank and Trust sets a mandatory transactional review threshold for all High risk accounts. Any single incoming or outgoing transaction exceeding forty-two thousand five hundred dollars requires an automated systemic halt and manual review by the alert disposition team to confirm the economic consistency of the transaction with the established customer profile.

Know Your Customer obligations at Cortex Bank and Trust do not conclude at onboarding, as ongoing monitoring is critical to maintaining an accurate understanding of client behavior. We mandate periodic file refreshes strictly governed by the assigned customer risk rating. High risk customer profiles must undergo a comprehensive manual review and documentation refresh every nine months. Standard risk profiles require a review every twenty-four months, while Low risk profiles are subject to review every thirty-six months. Regardless of the scheduled review cycle, an immediate out-of-cycle review is triggered upon any material change in account behavior. A material change is defined internally as sudden spikes in transaction volumes exceeding projected behavior by forty percent, unexplained changes in beneficial ownership, or a customer relocating to a heightened-risk jurisdiction. Finally, all Know Your Customer records, including identification documents, risk assessment rationales, beneficial ownership registries, and transaction histories, must be retained securely in the encrypted enterprise repository of Cortex Bank and Trust for a minimum period of eight years following the formal termination of the customer relationship.
