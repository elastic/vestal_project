The purpose of this section is to establish the framework utilized by Cortex Bank and Trust to identify, assess, and manage the money laundering and terrorist financing risks associated with Politically Exposed Persons. Cortex Bank and Trust recognizes that individuals holding prominent public positions, along with their immediate family members and known close associates, may be vulnerable to corruption, bribery, and the misappropriation of public funds. Therefore, Cortex Bank and Trust mandates stringent identification protocols and enhanced due diligence measures for all prospective and existing clients falling under this classification to ensure the uncompromised integrity of our financial ecosystem.

A Politically Exposed Person is defined by Cortex Bank and Trust as any natural person who is or has been entrusted with a prominent public function. This encompasses domestic political figures, foreign political figures, and individuals holding senior management roles within international organizations. Immediate family members, including spouses, civil partners, children, and parents, as well as known close associates who maintain joint beneficial ownership of legal entities or possess significant business relations with a primary Politically Exposed Person, are subject to identical regulatory scrutiny. Cortex Bank and Trust applies a perpetual risk approach, meaning an individual retains their elevated risk status for a minimum of thirty-six months after vacating their public office, subject to a formal declassification review and written approval by the Chief Compliance Officer.

To facilitate accurate identification during the customer onboarding phase, Cortex Bank and Trust employs a rigorous, multi-tiered screening methodology. Frontline personnel are required to administer a standardized Know Your Customer questionnaire that includes explicit self-declarations regarding political exposure. Concurrently, the compliance department routes all new customer data through the proprietary Cortex Bank and Trust Identity Verification system. This automated system cross-references applicant names against global watchlists, sanctions databases, and localized adverse media repositories. Any customer presenting a potential match is immediately flagged for manual adjudication by the Financial Intelligence Unit within Cortex Bank and Trust before any account can be formally opened or funded.

Upon the confirmed identification of a Politically Exposed Person, Cortex Bank and Trust imposes mandatory Enhanced Due Diligence procedures. This involves an exhaustive forensic examination of the individual's financial background. The designated relationship manager must obtain corroborated documentary evidence establishing both the source of wealth and the explicit source of funds for the specific account. Acceptable documentation includes audited financial statements, certified tax returns, or validated real estate conveyance records. Furthermore, establishing a business relationship with a confirmed Politically Exposed Person requires the explicit, documented authorization of the Cortex Bank and Trust Executive Risk Committee, which must be chaired by an officer no lower than the rank of Executive Vice President.

Cortex Bank and Trust has established specific financial thresholds to differentiate between standard and critically high-risk Politically Exposed Persons. A Politically Exposed Person is automatically escalated to critical risk status if their anticipated annual deposit volume exceeds two million five hundred thousand dollars, or if they initiate international wire transfers exceeding one hundred and fifty thousand dollars within a single rolling thirty-day period. Additionally, if the individual originates from or frequently transacts with a jurisdiction possessing a Cortex Bank and Trust internal risk score of seven or higher on our proprietary ten-point geopolitical risk scale, they are designated as critically high-risk. Such clients face stringent limitations, including a maximum daily transactional velocity limit of fifty thousand dollars and an absolute prohibition on the utilization of sweep accounts or offshore trust structures.

Ongoing monitoring of all accounts held by or linked to Politically Exposed Persons is a fundamental component of the Cortex Bank and Trust anti-money laundering framework. Standard Politically Exposed Person profiles are subject to comprehensive cyclical reviews every twelve months, whereas critically high-risk profiles must undergo formal reassessment every ninety days. The automated transaction monitoring architecture is specifically calibrated to apply enhanced algorithmic scrutiny to these accounts. Any deviation from the established baseline behavior, such as a sudden influx of funds from third-party corporate entities or an unexplained sequence of structured cash withdrawals nearing the ten thousand dollar reporting threshold, triggers an immediate high-priority automated alert. The Cortex Bank and Trust Financial Intelligence Unit is mandated to investigate and resolve these alerts within forty-eight hours of generation. Failure to adequately substantiate the economic rationale of flagged transactions will result in the immediate suspension of account privileges and the mandatory filing of a Suspicious Activity Report with the relevant financial intelligence authority.
