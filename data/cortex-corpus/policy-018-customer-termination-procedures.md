The purpose of this section is to establish the framework under which Cortex Bank and Trust will initiate the termination of a customer relationship due to anti-money laundering concerns, counter-terrorist financing risks, or sanctions compliance violations. Cortex Bank and Trust recognizes that certain customer relationships may present regulatory risks that exceed the institution's approved risk appetite, necessitating a formal, documented, and consistently applied exit strategy. This policy applies to all business lines and operational jurisdictions of Cortex Bank and Trust and supersedes any prior departmental procedures regarding compliance-driven account closures.

A recommendation for customer termination must be initiated when a customer meets or exceeds specific predetermined risk thresholds or behavioral triggers. Cortex Bank and Trust requires account closure review under the following circumstances. First, if a customer accumulates three or more Suspicious Activity Reports within a rolling twelve-month period, the relationship must be escalated for termination. Second, immediate termination review is required if a customer's enterprise risk score exceeds 850 on the Cortex Bank and Trust internal risk matrix. Third, failure by the customer to provide requested enhanced due diligence documentation within fifteen business days of the second formal request will trigger an automatic exit protocol. Additionally, any confirmed match against the Office of Foreign Assets Control Specially Designated Nationals list, or discovery of materially false information provided during the onboarding process, mandates immediate termination proceedings without a cure period.

The authority to terminate a customer relationship for compliance reasons rests exclusively with the Cortex Bank and Trust Customer Exit Committee. This committee convenes bi-weekly, or on an ad-hoc emergency basis, and is chaired by the Chief Bank Secrecy Act Officer. When a trigger event occurs, the primary investigator or account manager must submit a comprehensive termination memorandum to the committee. This memorandum must detail the nature of the identified risk, a summary of all transactional anomalies, the total financial exposure of Cortex Bank and Trust, and a record of all customer communications. The committee requires a unanimous vote from the Chief Bank Secrecy Act Officer, the Head of Enterprise Risk, and the relevant Line of Business Executive to finalize a termination order. Retail branch managers and relationship managers are strictly prohibited from independently initiating or reversing compliance-based closures.

Upon approval from the Customer Exit Committee, the closure process must be executed within thirty calendar days, barring extenuating circumstances. Cortex Bank and Trust will provide written notification to the customer via certified mail to the address on record, stating that the institution has elected to end the banking relationship. To mitigate legal risk and prevent tipping off the customer to ongoing investigations, the correspondence must utilize standardized boilerplate language citing general business decisions or policy changes, and must explicitly avoid referencing anti-money laundering investigations or the filing of Suspicious Activity Reports. The customer will be granted fourteen days from the date of the notice to make alternative banking arrangements. Following this period, Cortex Bank and Trust will freeze all associated accounts and disburse remaining consolidated balances via an official bank check mailed to the customer's verified address, provided the funds are not subject to a regulatory hold or asset freeze order.

There is one strict exception to the standard termination timeline. If Cortex Bank and Trust receives a formal, written keep-open request from a recognized law enforcement agency or regulatory body, the account will remain operational for the duration specified in the request, not to exceed ninety days without a formal renewal. In such cases, the account will be placed under continuous enhanced monitoring by the Financial Intelligence Unit. Once an account is ultimately closed, the former customer's identifying information must be permanently appended to the Cortex Bank and Trust internal prohibited entities registry to prevent future onboarding attempts across all corporate subsidiaries. All documentation pertaining to the exit decision, including committee minutes, investigative summaries, and outgoing correspondence, must be retained in the central compliance repository for a minimum of seven years following the date of final account closure to satisfy regulatory examination requirements.
