**Narrative**
This Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) is being filed by Cortex Bank and Trust to document a pattern of suspected structuring executed via daily ATM withdrawals by our customer, Elias Vance. The activity was initially flagged on October 12, 2023, by the bank’s automated transaction monitoring system due to an unusually high volume of cash withdrawals occurring shortly after the receipt of a large wire transfer.

On October 5, 2023, Mr. Vance’s personal Premier Checking account received a domestic wire transfer of $112,500 from a commercial entity identified as "Summit Holdings LLC." The payment reference simply stated "Consulting Fees." Prior to this deposit, the account maintained an average daily balance of approximately $4,500 and exhibited routine transactional behavior consisting of standard payroll deposits, utility payments, and everyday living expenses. 

Immediately following the clearing of the $112,500 wire, Mr. Vance commenced a pattern of aggressive cash withdrawals utilizing his Cortex Bank and Trust debit card. As a Premier account holder, Mr. Vance is granted an elevated daily ATM withdrawal limit of $10,000. Between October 6 and October 15, 2023, Mr. Vance conducted daily cash withdrawals exclusively through various out-of-network ATMs located in commercial districts across the greater Seattle area. 

The withdrawals were highly methodical and indicative of deliberate evasion tactics. On ten consecutive days, Mr. Vance withdrew aggregate daily amounts ranging precisely between $9,000 and $9,500. For example, on October 6, he completed nine separate $1,000 withdrawals at three different ATM terminals within a two-hour window. This identical pattern was repeated on October 7 ($9,500), October 8 ($9,000), October 9 ($9,400), and continued until a total of $94,000 in physical cash had been depleted from the account. By keeping the daily aggregate cash withdrawal amounts consistently below the $10,000 reporting threshold, the subject successfully bypassed the generation of any Currency Transaction Reports (CTRs).

On October 16, 2023, a Cortex Bank and Trust compliance investigator attempted to contact Mr. Vance as part of our standard Know Your Customer (KYC) outreach to verify the transactions and inquire about the economic purpose of the rapid cash depletion. Mr. Vance confirmed he authorized the withdrawals but became instantly defensive when asked about their purpose, vaguely stating he was "buying heavy equipment from private sellers" before abruptly terminating the call.

**Conclusion**
Cortex Bank and Trust is filing this SAR because the transaction pattern strongly indicates an intentional effort to structure cash withdrawals to evade federal Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) reporting requirements. The abrupt deviation from historical account behavior, the deliberate daily ATM withdrawal totals held just below the $10,000 CTR threshold, and the subject’s evasive response regarding the ultimate economic purpose of the cash collectively warrant high suspicion. Cortex Bank and Trust’s BSA/AML department has restricted the subject's debit card privileges, escalated the account for potential closure, and will continue to monitor the relationship in accordance with internal risk policies.
