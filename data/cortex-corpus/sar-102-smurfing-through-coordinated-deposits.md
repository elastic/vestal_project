**Narrative**
Cortex Bank and Trust’s Anti-Money Laundering (AML) automated monitoring system generated an alert on the Business Checking Account of Thorne Logistics LLC due to an anomalous velocity and volume of physical cash deposits. The account is primarily managed by Elias Thorne, with Marcus Vance acting as a secondary authorized signer. Historically, the customer's business profile indicated that the logistics company operated predominantly via Automated Clearing House (ACH) credits and domestic wire transfers. Monthly cash deposits historically rarely exceeded $2,500.

Beginning October 1, 2023, a highly coordinated pattern of rapid, high-value cash deposits commenced. Over an 18-day period, a total of $87,400 in physical currency was deposited into the account across ten separate transactions. Notably, every single deposit ranged between $8,100 and $9,600, strategically remaining just below the $10,000 regulatory reporting threshold to avoid triggering a Currency Transaction Report (CTR). 

A review of branch surveillance footage and teller logs revealed a classic "smurfing" typology. The deposits were systematically executed by multiple individuals across four different Cortex Bank and Trust branches within the metropolitan area. For instance, on October 4, 2023, Elias Thorne deposited $9,200 at the Downtown branch at 10:15 AM. Less than two hours later, at 11:45 AM, Marcus Vance deposited $8,900 at the Westside branch. The following day, an unidentified female individual deposited $9,500 at the North Hills branch, referencing the Thorne Logistics LLC account number on a physical deposit slip. 

This geographic dispersion and the utilization of multiple conductors on concurrent days appear explicitly designed to circumvent daily cash aggregation limits and evade institutional detection. Furthermore, the sudden influx of nearly $90,000 in physical cash does not align with the stated operational profile of a mid-sized freight logistics firm. 

Upon clearing, the deposited funds were rapidly dissipated. Between October 5 and October 20, 2023, Elias Thorne initiated four outgoing wire transfers totaling $85,000 to a foreign corporate bank account held by "Apex International Trading" in Cyprus. The rapid placement of structured physical cash into the financial system, followed immediately by the layering of funds via international wire transfers, presents a severe risk for illicit money laundering.

**Conclusion**
Cortex Bank and Trust is filing this Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) due to the strong indicators of smurfing and coordinated structuring designed to evade CTR reporting requirements under the Bank Secrecy Act. The volume of physical currency is fundamentally inconsistent with the known business practices of Thorne Logistics LLC, and the subsequent offshore wire transfers further elevate the risk of illicit financial activity. In accordance with Cortex Bank and Trust’s internal risk protocols, Thorne Logistics LLC’s account has been placed under a strict debit restriction to prevent further capital flight, and the customer relationship is currently undergoing an administrative review for imminent closure.
