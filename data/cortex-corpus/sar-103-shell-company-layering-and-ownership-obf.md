**Activity Overview**
**Suspicious Activity Period:** March 1, 2026 – July 15, 2026 
**Total Transaction Volume:** Approximately $3.45 million 
**Summary:** Cortex Bank and Trust is filing this Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) to detail a complex network of transactions involving multiple limited liability companies. The activity is characterized by the rapid layering of funds, the use of apparent shell companies, and the obfuscation of ultimate beneficial ownership through offshore trust structures. The transaction patterns exhibit no clear economic or commercial purpose.

**Narrative**
On June 12, 2026, Cortex Bank and Trust’s automated transaction monitoring system generated an alert regarding rapid fund velocity and unusually high wire transfer volumes in the business checking account (x4452) of Apex Global Holdings LLC. The account was opened in January 2026, ostensibly to function as a domestic supply chain logistics provider. 

Upon review, Cortex Bank and Trust investigators identified a sophisticated layering phase orchestrating the movement of approximately $3.45 million over a four-month period. Apex Global Holdings LLC received high-frequency, round-dollar incoming domestic wire transfers ranging from $50,000 to $150,000. These originating entities were primarily domestic, notably Orion Trading Partners Inc. and Zenith Consulting LLC, both of which also hold business accounts at Cortex Bank and Trust. 

Once deposited, the funds were typically held for less than 48 hours before being consolidated and wired internationally. The primary beneficiary of these outgoing international wires was Vanguard Logistics Ltd., an entity maintaining bank accounts in Cyprus. 

Enhanced due diligence revealed significant red flags regarding ownership obfuscation. The account opening documentation for Apex Global Holdings LLC lists Marcus Thorne as a 15% owner. The remaining 85% is controlled by a corporate entity, Blue Horizon Trust, domiciled in the British Virgin Islands, effectively shielding the ultimate beneficial owners from standard regulatory scrutiny. Furthermore, internal link analysis confirmed that Marcus Thorne is also the sole authorized signer for Orion Trading Partners Inc. The business addresses for Apex Global, Orion Trading, and Zenith Consulting all trace back to the exact same registered agent address in Wilmington, Delaware, with no evidence of a physical operational presence. 

Additionally, an analysis of the account statements across all associated entities revealed a complete absence of standard business operating expenses. There were no disbursements for payroll, utilities, insurance, or taxes. The accounts operated strictly as pass-through vehicles, pooling domestic funds and funneling them to an offshore jurisdiction under the guise of inter-company logistical payments.

**Conclusion**
Cortex Bank and Trust assesses that Marcus Thorne is utilizing a network of domestic shell companies to orchestrate a layering scheme designed to obfuscate both the origin of the funds and the identity of the true beneficial owners. The activity lacks a legitimate, verifiable business purpose, funnels capital to high-risk offshore jurisdictions, and utilizes nested corporate structures typical of advanced money laundering typologies. Due to the severe and opaque nature of the suspicious activity, Cortex Bank and Trust is filing this SAR, has initiated the permanent closure of all accounts associated with Marcus Thorne and the identified corporate entities, and has restricted all international wire capabilities during the 30-day exit process.
