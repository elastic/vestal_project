**Activity Overview**
Between March 1, 2026, and July 15, 2026, Cortex Bank and Trust identified highly suspicious wire transfer activity totaling approximately $7.9 million ($4.0 million in incoming credits and $3.9 million in outgoing debits) flowing through the business checking account of Zenith Horizon Logistics LLC. The activity is characterized by rapid pass-through of funds, significant deviations from the anticipated account profile, and the suspected use of a nominee director to intentionally obscure the Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO) of the corporate entity.

**Narrative**
Zenith Horizon Logistics LLC opened its primary operating account with Cortex Bank and Trust in November 2025. During the initial onboarding process, Customer Due Diligence (CDD) documents indicated the company would act as a regional freight broker, with expected annual revenues of $750,000 and primarily domestic transaction counterparts.

On February 20, 2026, the company submitted an ownership structure update to Cortex Bank and Trust, removing the original founders and listing Mr. Elias Thorne as the sole director and 100% beneficial owner. Shortly after this structural change, the account behavior shifted dramatically. Beginning in March 2026, the account began receiving high-value incoming wire transfers in even-dollar amounts (ranging from $150,000 to $400,000) from shell companies located in Cyprus and the British Virgin Islands. Within 24 to 48 hours of receipt, these funds were subsequently wired out to various manufacturing and export firms in Southeast Asia. 

Cortex Bank and Trust’s Anti-Money Laundering (AML) investigation unit initiated a review due to the rapid, pass-through nature of the funds and the international origin of the wires, which directly contradicted the stated domestic business model. A background investigation into the sole director, Elias Thorne, revealed significant red flags indicating he is acting as a nominee director. Public records show Mr. Thorne is an 83-year-old retired schoolteacher residing in an assisted living facility in rural Ohio. Furthermore, corporate registry databases revealed that Mr. Thorne’s name and signature are currently listed as the primary director for over forty distinct, unrelated corporate entities across Delaware, Wyoming, and Nevada. 

When Cortex Bank and Trust requested supporting documentation—such as commercial invoices, bills of lading, and an updated UBO declaration—the responses were highly irregular. The provided invoices lacked standard tax identification numbers and featured formatting inconsistencies indicative of fraudulent fabrication. Additionally, the metadata and IP addresses associated with the client’s email responses originated from a high-risk jurisdiction in Eastern Europe, rather than Delaware or Ohio. 

**Conclusion**
Cortex Bank and Trust is filing this Suspicious Activity Report based on the strong likelihood that Zenith Horizon Logistics LLC is a shell corporation utilizing a nominee director network to conceal its true beneficial ownership. The lack of legitimate business footprint, combined with Mr. Thorne’s prolific directorships and the unexplained multi-million-dollar pass-through wire activity, strongly suggests the account is being utilized to facilitate international money laundering or to bypass global financial sanctions. In accordance with internal risk mitigation policies, Cortex Bank and Trust has placed a hard freeze on the account and is in the process of terminating the banking relationship.
