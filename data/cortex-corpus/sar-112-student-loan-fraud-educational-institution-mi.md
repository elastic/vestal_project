**SUBJECT INFORMATION**
**Primary Suspect Entity:** Apex Tech Academy LLC (DBA: Apex Vocational) 
**Primary Suspect Individual:** Marcus T. Vance (Owner/Signer)
**Secondary Suspect Individual:** Elena Rostova (Account Signer/Administrator)
**Account Relationships:** Cortex Bank and Trust Business Checking ending in #4092, Personal Checking ending in #8115, Personal Checking ending in #3321.

**ACTIVITY OVERVIEW**
Between March 1, 2026, and July 15, 2026, Cortex Bank and Trust identified suspicious activity involving Apex Tech Academy LLC. The activity is characterized by the receipt of private student loan disbursements totaling approximately $485,000, followed by rapid, atypical depletion of funds. The transactional behavior and subsequent due diligence strongly suggest that Apex Tech Academy LLC is a shell entity engaged in student loan fraud through educational institution misrepresentation. 

**DETAILED NARRATIVE**
Apex Tech Academy LLC opened a Business Checking account (#4092) at Cortex Bank and Trust on January 14, 2026, listing its primary business as "IT and Cybersecurity Vocational Training." Marcus T. Vance was listed as the sole owner, with Elena Rostova as an authorized signer. 

Account activity remained dormant until March 3, 2026, when the business account began receiving high volumes of incoming ACH credits from three prominent private student loan servicers. Over a four-month period, the account received 42 separate ACH credits ranging from $9,500 to $18,000, totaling $485,250. The ACH addenda records indicated these funds were tuition disbursements for various individual students.

Cortex Bank and Trust’s AML monitoring system flagged the account due to the rapid velocity of outbound transfers immediately following the receipt of these loan funds. Within 24 to 48 hours of each loan disbursement, Vance and Rostova initiated outbound transfers that were entirely inconsistent with the operation of a legitimate educational institution. There were no outgoing payments for payroll, facility leasing, educational software, or instructional supplies. Instead, 85% of the funds were transferred to Vance’s personal checking account (#8115). 

Once in Vance’s personal account, the funds were dissipated through two primary methods. Approximately $250,000 was wired to an offshore cryptocurrency exchange (CoinHorizon Ltd.) in the Cayman Islands. An additional $115,000 was withdrawn in cash through a series of structured ATM and branch withdrawals ranging from $8,500 to $9,500, appearing designed to evade Currency Transaction Report (CTR) thresholds. 

Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) conducted by Cortex Bank and Trust’s Financial Intelligence Unit revealed significant discrepancies indicating institutional misrepresentation. The physical address provided for Apex Tech Academy is a mail-drop location in a strip mall, not a viable campus or classroom facility. A review of the institution's website revealed it was registered on February 20, 2026, utilizing stock imagery, non-functional enrollment links, and listing fabricated regional accreditations. Furthermore, open-source intelligence indicates that several of the "students" listed in the ACH addenda records correspond with known synthetic identity profiles flagged in recent credit bureau alerts.

**CONCLUSION / ACTION**
Cortex Bank and Trust assesses that Marcus T. Vance and Elena Rostova have established a fictitious educational institution to systematically defraud private student lenders. Consequently, Cortex Bank and Trust has initiated the closure of Apex Tech Academy LLC’s Business Checking (#4092) and Vance’s Personal Checking (#8115), effectively restricting approximately $42,000 in remaining funds pending final account resolution. 

Pursuant to Section 314(b) information sharing and internal escalation, a proactive referral has been made to the local FBI Financial Crimes Task Force. At the direct written request of law enforcement, Elena Rostova’s Personal Checking account (#3321) remains open and is subject to 14-day cycle targeted monitoring to assist in their ongoing investigation into the wider synthetic identity ring.
