**SUBJECT INFORMATION**
**Suspect/Beneficiary:** Unknown individual(s) utilizing the alias "David Aris Thorne" and corporate shell "Crestview Global Logistics Ltd." 
**Victim/Customer:** Eleanor M. Higgins (DOB: 04/12/1951)
**Account Relationships:** Cortex Bank and Trust Premium Checking (...4492) and Money Market Savings (...1105)

**ACTIVITY OVERVIEW**
Cortex Bank and Trust’s AML Investigations Unit initiated a review of customer Eleanor M. Higgins following an internal referral from the retail branch network regarding uncharacteristic and high-risk international wire transfers. Between February 12, 2024, and April 5, 2024, Ms. Higgins initiated or attempted to initiate a series of outgoing international wire transfers totaling $95,000.00. The activity is highly indicative of a combined Romance and Advance Fee Fraud scheme, wherein the customer was manipulated into sending funds to foreign beneficiaries under the guise of paying administrative fees to release a larger fictitious financial payout.

**DETAILED NARRATIVE**
Prior to February 2024, Ms. Higgins’s account activity was entirely domestic, consisting primarily of fixed-income deposits, Social Security credits, and routine point-of-sale expenditures. 

The suspicious activity commenced on February 12, 2024, when Ms. Higgins visited the Cortex Bank and Trust downtown branch to request an international wire transfer of $15,000.00 to a personal account at Ziraat Bank in Istanbul, Turkey, listing the beneficiary as "David Aris Thorne." When questioned about the purpose of the wire by the branch manager, Ms. Higgins stated the funds were for "emergency medical expenses" for a friend working on an overseas oil rig. The wire was processed as requested.

On March 8, 2024, Ms. Higgins liquidated her $100,000.00 Certificate of Deposit, incurring an early withdrawal penalty, and transferred the funds into her Premium Checking account. The following day, she requested a second international wire for $45,000.00. This wire was directed to a corporate account held at Barclays Bank PLC in London, United Kingdom, under the beneficiary name "Crestview Global Logistics Ltd." The memo line stated: "Customs Clearance Fee." 

On April 5, 2024, Ms. Higgins attempted to initiate a third wire transfer in the amount of $35,000.00 to the same UK corporate account. At this time, branch personnel escalated the interaction, noting that Ms. Higgins appeared highly distressed and was actively messaging an unknown individual on her mobile device. During an extended interview with the Branch Manager and the Regional Fraud Officer, Ms. Higgins disclosed that she had been in a romantic online relationship with "David" for five months. She stated "David" was an American civil engineer who had secured a $4.5 million contract but required advance fee payments to foreign customs and logistics authorities to release the funds, after which he intended to return to the United States to marry her.

**CONCLUSION/ACTION**
Cortex Bank and Trust successfully intercepted and rejected the final $35,000.00 wire attempt on April 5, 2024, citing suspected fraud. However, the initial $60,000.00 sent in February and March was successfully transmitted and is deemed unrecoverable. 

Due to the clear indicators of Elder Financial Exploitation (EFE) and international wire fraud, Cortex Bank and Trust has placed a permanent block on all outgoing wire capabilities for Ms. Higgins’s accounts to prevent further asset depletion. The Bank provided Ms. Higgins with comprehensive fraud education materials. In accordance with state regulations, Cortex Bank and Trust has filed a mandatory report with State Adult Protective Services. Furthermore, this SAR and the associated transaction data are being forwarded directly to the FBI’s Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3) for further law enforcement investigation regarding the overseas beneficiary accounts.
