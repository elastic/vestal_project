**Activity Overview**
Between February 1, 2026, and June 30, 2026, Cortex Bank and Trust identified suspicious wire transfer activity indicative of Trade-Based Money Laundering (TBML), specifically involving phantom shipping and Bill of Lading (BoL) manipulation. During the review period, Apex Horizon Trading LLC received $4.2 million in domestic originations, which were subsequently remitted via 18 outgoing international wire transfers to a single beneficiary in a high-risk jurisdiction. The transactions are wholly inconsistent with the Subject’s historical account profile and appear supported by fraudulent maritime shipping documentation. 

**Detailed Narrative**
The investigation was initiated following an automated transaction monitoring alert for high-velocity international wire transfers. A review of the Subject’s Commercial Business Checking account revealed a distinct pass-through funding pattern. Apex Horizon Trading LLC received bi-weekly incoming domestic wire transfers, typically in even, round-dollar amounts ranging from $150,000 to $300,000, originating from newly established, opaque corporate entities registered in Delaware and Wyoming. 

Within 24 to 48 hours of clearing, these funds were remitted internationally to "Crescent Logistics FZE," a company maintaining an account at a regional bank in the United Arab Emirates. The remittance details consistently referenced "Payment for Heavy Machinery Invoices."

To substantiate the underlying economic purpose of these cross-border payments, Cortex Bank and Trust’s Trade Finance Compliance division issued a formal Request for Information (RFI) to the Subject on May 15, 2026. The Subject provided four Bills of Lading (BoLs) corresponding to outgoing wires totaling $950,000. 

Upon enhanced due diligence review, Cortex Bank and Trust investigators identified severe discrepancies in the provided documentation, confirming the likelihood of phantom shipping:
1.  **Vessel Discrepancy:** All four BoLs cited the maritime vessel *MV Atlantic Star* loading cargo at the Port of Savannah, Georgia, on dates between April 10 and May 5, 2026. Open-source maritime tracking (AIS data) confirmed that the *MV Atlantic Star* was actively docked in Piraeus, Greece, during this entire timeframe.
2.  **Document Alteration:** The carrier stamps and authorized signatures on the BoLs appeared digitally superimposed. Furthermore, the container tracking numbers listed on the documents were verified against the International Bureau of Containers (BIC) registry and found to be invalid or assigned to scrapped units.
3.  **Template Duplication:** Despite representing four distinct shipments of varying goods (excavators vs. industrial generators), the gross weight and volumetric measurements on three of the four BoLs were identical down to the decimal point, strongly suggesting the use of a forged template.

No physical movement of goods appears to have occurred, indicating the BoLs were manipulated solely to justify the illicit transfer of $4.2 million out of the United States. 

**Conclusion/Action**
Due to the egregious nature of the forged documentation and the severe risk of facilitating Trade-Based Money Laundering, Cortex Bank and Trust has placed a hard freeze on all accounts associated with Apex Horizon Trading LLC and its beneficial owner, Alistair Vance. The bank is currently in the 30-day process of formally exiting the client relationship. Given the utilization of domestic shell companies and the international flow of potentially illicit funds, Cortex Bank and Trust has proactively referred this case to Homeland Security Investigations (HSI) - Financial Crimes Task Force for further criminal inquiry. This matter remains ongoing pending final account closure.
