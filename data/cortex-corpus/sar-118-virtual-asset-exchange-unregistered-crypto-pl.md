**SUBJECT INFORMATION**
**Subject Entity:** Horizon Tech Solutions LLC (dba HorizonPay)
**Principal/Beneficial Owner:** Marcus T. Vance
**Account Involved:** Cortex Bank and Trust Business Checking ending in #8841
**Account Open Date:** February 12, 2023
**Stated Business Type:** B2B Software Development and IT Consulting 
**Risk Rating at Onboarding:** Low

**ACTIVITY OVERVIEW**
Cortex Bank and Trust is filing this Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) to document the activities of Horizon Tech Solutions LLC and its principal, Marcus T. Vance. Between August 1, 2023, and November 30, 2023, the subject account exhibited transaction patterns highly indicative of operating an unregistered Money Services Business (MSB), specifically an unregistered Virtual Asset Service Provider (VASP). During the review period, the account processed approximately $3.85 million in aggregate pass-through transaction volume that severely deviated from the customer’s stated business profile.

**DETAILED NARRATIVE**
At onboarding, Marcus T. Vance represented Horizon Tech Solutions LLC as a domestic IT consulting firm, anticipating monthly transaction volumes of $50,000 to $80,000, primarily consisting of standard corporate ACH payments and payroll disbursements. From account inception through July 2023, the account operated commensurately with this profile.

Beginning in early August 2023, Cortex Bank and Trust’s automated transaction monitoring system generated alerts for a sudden, exponential increase in rapid fund movements. Analysts observed a high volume of incoming ACH credits and domestic wire transfers originating from over 400 distinct individual consumer accounts across various financial institutions. Individual deposit amounts ranged from $500 to $15,000. Notably, the memo lines for these incoming transactions frequently contained terminology associated with cryptocurrency, such as "BTC purchase," "crypto wallet reload," "ETH swap," and "Horizon Virtual." 

Following the placement of these funds, the account exhibited rapid outflow patterns indicative of layering. Within 24 to 48 hours of receipt, the aggregated funds were wired out in large, round-dollar amounts (typically between $100,000 and $250,000) to well-known virtual currency exchanges, including offshore entities. Outbound wire beneficiaries included "Apex Digital Asset Exchange LTD" and "Global Coin Vault Inc." 

On October 15, 2023, Cortex Bank and Trust’s Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) team initiated a Request for Information (RFI) to Mr. Vance regarding the anomalous activity. Mr. Vance claimed the funds were related to a "new payment gateway software" his company was testing. However, open-source intelligence and negative news screening conducted by the bank’s AML investigators revealed that Horizon Tech Solutions was operating a public-facing website (HorizonPay.io) offering peer-to-peer virtual asset exchange services and fiat-to-crypto onboarding. 

A review of the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) MSB Registrant Search confirmed that neither Horizon Tech Solutions LLC nor Marcus T. Vance is registered as an MSB. Furthermore, the entity lacks the necessary state-level money transmitter licenses required for the jurisdictions in which it appears to be operating.

**CONCLUSION/ACTION**
Due to the significant regulatory risk posed by the operation of an unregistered virtual asset exchange, Cortex Bank and Trust has made the decision to exit this relationship. On November 28, 2023, the bank placed a hard post-no-debit restriction on the Business Checking account ending in #8841. The account was formally closed on December 5, 2023, and a cashier’s check for the remaining balance of $42,118.55 was mailed to the address of record. Cortex Bank and Trust’s Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) has escalated this case and referred the findings to the local field office of the FBI Cyber Crimes Task Force for further investigation regarding potential illicit money transmission.
