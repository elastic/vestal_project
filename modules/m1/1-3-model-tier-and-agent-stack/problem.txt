Q: What is Cortex Bank's internal threshold for filing a Suspicious Activity Report when the
   suspect is known, and is a $4,000 suspicious transfer by a known customer reportable?
Stage 1 recorded. Tina says:
Cortex Bank's internal threshold for filing a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) depends on the nature of the suspect:

1.  **Known Customer (non-insider):** The internal threshold for filing a SAR when the suspect is a known customer (not an officer, director, employee, or agent of the bank) is **$5,000 or more** for transactions that involve or aggregate to that amount and are deemed suspicious.
2.  **Insider Abuse:** If the suspect is an insider (e.g., officer, director, employee, agent, or other institution-affiliated party), the threshold for filing a SAR is **any amount**, regardless of how small the transaction.

Therefore, a single **$4,000 suspicious transfer by a known customer** is generally **not reportable** under the mandatory FinCEN SAR filing thresholds, as it falls below the $5,000 threshold for non-insider suspicious activity.
