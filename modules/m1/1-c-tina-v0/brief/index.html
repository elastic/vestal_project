Capstone 1.C: Build Tina v0

Cortex Bank and Trust's compliance analysts are about to start asking Tina policy questions that need two facts each. You build the first version of Tina that can answer them, inside a budget, and then defend how you routed her between the two model tiers.

The scenario

The cortex-policies index holds Cortex's anti-money-laundering policies. Tina's harness is installed with its tool registry and ReAct loop, but no tools are registered, so today she cannot search anything. Three held-out questions are waiting. Each needs two facts, from one policy or from two.

Cortex pays for every token, and the strong tier costs more than the fast tier. An analyst also needs to see which policy each fact came from.
