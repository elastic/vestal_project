A named endpoint records the model

A regulator can ask which model produced an answer. The platform's dot-prefixed endpoints are managed by Elastic, so which ones exist and when their models retire is not in your config. A named endpoint records the service and model you chose in your own config.

EndpointWhose configWhat it records
Dot-prefixed, preconfiguredElastic'sElastic manages the set and its lifecycle
cortex-generationYoursThe service and model_id you chose, for audit

Pinning does not outlive a model's end-of-life date: after it, calls fail and you move to another model.

Decision rule

Take the first row that applies.

What done looks like

  • Median chunk: at most 512 tokens
  • Largest chunk: at most 2,048 tokens
  • Structured units kept whole: at least 3 of 4
  • Procedure questions with the whole answer in one chunk of the top 5: at least 3 of 5
  • Policy questions with the whole answer in one chunk of the top 5: at least 4 of 6
  • No question's top 5 over Tina's 4,000-token budget
  • cortex-corpus-live resolves to cortex-corpus
  • cortex-generation exists, pinned to the platform model
  • At least 3 traces, the latest three all through cortex-generation
  • At least 2 of 3 held-out questions answered with the value the policy states
