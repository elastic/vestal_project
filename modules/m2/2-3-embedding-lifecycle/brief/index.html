Lab 2.3: Embedding lifecycle

A new embedding model is available. Before it ships, you must benchmark it, cut over without downtime, and raise recall for the queries it still gets wrong.

The problem

An analyst asks Tina, in their own words:

query:     money moved around to hide where it came from
target:    sar-103  (shell company layering and ownership obfuscation)
retrieved: sar-109, sar-105, sar-116, sar-110

Based on the documents provided, several cases describe money being moved
around to hide its origin:

**Loan Layering** [sar-109]: Vance deposited funds into a CD, then borrowed
against them via a Line of Credit and immediately wired the proceeds to
third parties ...

The case that answers it, sar-103, is not in Tina's top 5. The query has none of its words: layering, shell company, ownership. A new embedding model is ready to test, and cortex-corpus-live must stay searchable while you move to it.

What you will do

  • Build 1 — Benchmark: Write a labelled benchmark of at least 12 queries covering every document type. The check measures nDCG@5 and p95 latency on both models.
  • Build 2 — Cut over: Swap cortex-corpus-live to the candidate index in one atomic _aliases call, with no failed or empty search.
  • Build 3 — Raise recall: Write rewrite() so that at least 8 of 13 queries keyword search misses today find their target.
  • Defend: Decide whether to keep the cutover or roll it back, given the gain, noise band, and latency budget.

Deployment decision framework

Gain over currentCandidate p95Decision
More than 0.02Within budgetKeep the cutover
0.02 or lessWithin budgetRoll back: no real gain
More than 0.02Over budgetRoll back: too slow
0.02 or lessOver budgetRoll back: both

Key concepts

  • nDCG@5 — ranking quality metric; higher is better
  • Noise band (0.02) — a gain of 0.02 or less is measurement noise
  • Atomic alias swap — remove + add in one request; zero downtime
  • Query-side rewriting — fix recall without touching the index
  • HyDE — ask a model for the passage that would answer the query, then search with that passage
  • cortex-generation — Tina's completion endpoint, pinned in lab 2.1: es.inference.inference(inference_id="cortex-generation", task_type="completion", body={"input": prompt})["completion"][0]["result"]
  • llm_client() — the same models through an OpenAI-style client: from tina.client import llm_client, model_fast, then llm_client().chat.completions.create(model=model_fast(), messages=[{"role": "user", "content": prompt}]).choices[0].message.content

What done looks like

  • Benchmark set: at least 12 labelled queries, covering policy, sar and wire-fraud
  • cortex-corpus-candidate embeds with the candidate endpoint
  • cortex-corpus-live points only to cortex-corpus-candidate; cortex-corpus is kept
  • Probe during the move: at least 20 samples, 0 failed and 0 empty searches
  • Live index mapping and document count unchanged
  • rewrite(): at least 8 of 13 held-out queries keyword search misses find their target in the first 5 documents, all calls within 35 seconds
  • Defend: gains of 0.02 or less are noise; your p95 budget is 300 or 500 ms
