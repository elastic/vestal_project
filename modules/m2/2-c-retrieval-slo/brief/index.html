Capstone 2.C: Retrieval SLO

Cortex Bank's compliance assistant Tina has a retrieval service-level objective. Today's pipeline doesn't meet it. You diagnose why Tina misses, pull the levers that fix it within the SLO, and defend your choice.

The scenario

Tina's answers to compliance questions come back incomplete. Today, for 0 of 4 held-out policy questions and 1 of 4 case questions, one chunk in Tina's top 5 holds the whole answer. The SLO requires 3 of 4 in each. Finding out why is your first job.

What you will do

  • Diagnose: Run /home/elastic/.venv/bin/python /home/elastic/submit.py before you change anything. It prints the cause of each dev miss: split, or retrieval miss
  • Improve: Edit /home/elastic/retriever.json and/or re-chunk into a new index and cut the alias over
  • Evaluate: Re-run submit.py — iterate until at least 3 of 4 dev questions pass in each class
  • Defend: Run python3 /opt/ara/lib/defend.py to record your answers, then select Check

Grading criteria

ARTIFACT cortex-corpus-live alias resolves; retriever.json is valid and runs against it; embedding model unchanged; body_text mapped as text

MEASURED p95 ≤ 800 ms; chunk count ≤ 1.5× baseline (nDCG@10 logged as a diagnostic)

BEHAVIOR One chunk in Tina's top 5 holds the whole answer for ≥ 3 of 4 held-out policy questions and ≥ 3 of 4 case questions; no top 5 over 4,000 tokens

DEFEND Your answers match the diagnosis and your live configuration
