Cortex Bank's compliance assistant Tina has a retrieval service-level objective. Today's pipeline doesn't meet it. You diagnose why Tina misses, pull the levers that fix it within the SLO, and defend your choice.

The scenario

Cortex's regional deployment never got the fixes from labs 2.1 to 2.3. There Tina answers from a 200-token fixed-chunk index through a dense-only retriever.

Her answers to compliance questions come back incomplete. Today, for 0 of 4 held-out policy questions and 1 of 4 case questions, one chunk in Tina's top 5 holds the whole answer. The SLO requires 3 of 4 in each. Finding out why is your first job.
