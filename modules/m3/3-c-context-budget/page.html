Cortex Bank and Trust's compliance desk puts about twenty questions an hour to Tina. Every answer is read by an analyst who acts on it.

The scenario

The desk's questions mix three sources: the policy library, the case files and the SAR narratives. Some ask for something the corpus does not hold, and the desk cannot tell which before it asks. A figure Tina invents costs more than an answer she declines to give: the analyst acts on the first and looks up the second.
