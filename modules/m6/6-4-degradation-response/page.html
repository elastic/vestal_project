Lab 6.4: Detect and respond to degradation

Thirty days of canary records are in the index, and Tina is worse today. Find the three incident windows in the history, then find and fix the one happening now.

Tip: Select Hide Instructions in the top bar to give the Brief full width.

The problem

The series stepped down on one day. The change log shows a model pin the same afternoon. The pin changed no model. The step came from somewhere else.

Three kinds of degradation

Retrieval drift: a step down in ranking metrics after an ingest, under both semantic and lexical retrieval, with the new documents in the top results; model, tools and routes unchanged. Embedding staleness: documents embedded under different models, or a different model than the queries. Scores do not compare, older documents fall out of semantic results, and lexical search still finds them. Behavior shift: the tool-call mix moves and escalations rise while retrieval stays flat, usually after an instruction, description or model change.

Keep the series that tell them apart

The canary records are the series: routing accuracy by category, tool calls per question, escalation share, recall and nDCG at 5, and a lexical control, recall at 5 under cortex-policy-lexical. The lexical series is why staleness is visible: it holds while the semantic one falls. Run it with search_type=dfs_query_then_fetch: without it, BM25 scores differ per index, so splitting the data across two indices moves the lexical series too.

CHANGE_POINT

CHANGE_POINT finds where a series changes. Types: step_change, spike, dip, distribution_change, trend_change. It needs at least 22 values per series; use BY to group.

A change log is a lead, not a verdict

Correlate a change point with the change log without trusting the log: it holds changes that did not cause the incident, sometimes closer in time than the cause that did. The evidence is the series and the traces.

The playbook

Diagnose (compare with a baseline from the same environment), isolate (component and affected class), fix, re-measure. A recovery inside the run-to-run spread is not a recovery.

How a semantic_text field records its endpoint

A semantic_text field keeps its inference_id in the mapping, and the endpoint's model and version come from the inference API. Updating inference_id is only allowed to a compatible endpoint, so a mixed set is finished by re-indexing, not by editing the mapping: an update to an endpoint with different model settings is refused (HTTP 400).

Decision rule

Take the first row that applies.

What done looks like

  • Build 1: three windows with type, signal and a date within one day.
  • Build 2: the component and class named, the cause fixed with nothing else changed, and a re-measure within tolerance of the baseline.
  • Defend: the signal, why the log misleads, and what the re-measure catches.
