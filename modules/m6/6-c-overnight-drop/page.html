Capstone 6.C: Architect's Challenge, overnight drop

The 06:00 canary run shows a drop and the desk opens soon. Find the cause from the evidence, put the fix through the gate before applying it, and leave a monitor that would have fired before the morning.

Tip: Select Hide Instructions in the top bar to give the Brief full width.

The scenario

Cortex's fraud desk works in America/New_York. Shifts start at 06:00, 14:00 and 22:00, and the desk opens at 07:00. The change policy is in ~/cortex-loop/change-policy.md and its reference gate is gate.json, with an incident block.

The canary has run three times a day for thirty days. Last night's 22:00 run was healthy. The 06:00 run was not. The change log holds last night's entries, and one of them is not the cause.
